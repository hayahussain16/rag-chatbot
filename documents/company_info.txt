Company Information

Our company follows a hybrid work model.

Employees work from Monday to Friday.

The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees receive 20 days of paid leave per year.

The company has four main departments: Engineering, Data Science, Human Resources, and Marketing.

The Engineering department develops and maintains software applications.

The Data Science department works on data analysis, machine learning, and artificial intelligence projects.

The Human Resources department handles recruitment, employee benefits, and workplace policies.

The Marketing department manages campaigns, branding, and customer communication.
